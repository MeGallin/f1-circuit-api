## Meaning

- Current security uses strict points, or proven race-wins-only dominance at equal
  points: leader wins must exceed rival wins plus remaining races. Valid published
  wins are bounded by completed races; their supplied sum cannot exceed that scope.
  Sprint wins do not count. Equal wins and later FIA A2.1.4 stages stay unresolved.
- Next-event conditions assume a leader win or constructor1–2, checking every alive
  published rival and an explicit unlisted bound from trusted rank2 when partial.
  Limits are sufficient, not exhaustive. Null is not proven, not elimination.
  One driver wins; team rivals use distinct remaining car slots, not driver logic.
- Earliest possibility constructs leader wins/all other competitors zero, checking
  every future rival ceiling. An earlier unresolved tie changes the label to earliest
  **proven path**, not an exact earliest claim.
- Constant-gap threshold is hypothetical: **Assumes the leader maintains at least
  the current gap over every challenger; the nearest rival may change.** It does not
  invent jointly feasible results or track only today's second-ranked competitor.
- Winning pattern uses leaderfirst/nearestsecond, or teams1–2/3–4. Other rivals are
  checked against independent upper bounds from the best remaining places, not
  simultaneous allocations of those places. This is a sufficient guaranteed path.

The original one-point-margin sufficient target is unchanged and does not resolve
countback or an exact minimum. Only scenarios may prove wins-only dominance; no
full countback/combinatorial solver. Full points, unchanged rules, no new events or
penalties are explicit assumptions. Cancelled events are excluded. Chronology
requires real owned-year dates and rejects contradictory/ambiguous ordering.
Unavailable reasons follow the actual failed evidence scope. Optional legacy/null
transport is supported. Existing stable standings ETags include projected data.
